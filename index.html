How to Take a Screenshot (Windows)

  1. Press Windows key + Shift + S to open the Snipping Tool.
  2. Select the area of the screen you want to capture.
  3. The screenshot is copied to your clipboard.
  4. Paste (Ctrl + V) into a document, email, or assignment.
  5. Alternatively, press PrtScn to capture the full screen and paste it.
